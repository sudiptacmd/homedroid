How to customize the Homedroid dashboard: layouts, themes and fonts

The Homedroid dashboard doesn’t have to look one way. Pick one of six layouts and 13 themes, change the fonts, or start from a preset. Everything is saved on the phone, so the dashboard looks the same in every browser you use.

Before you start

  • A running Homedroid server and the dashboard open in a browser. Nothing to install.
  • Changes apply as you click. Reset on the Customize dialog puts everything back, and Export and Import move your setup to another phone.

How to customize the dashboard: step by step

  1. Open Customize

    Select the sliders button at the top right of the dashboard, or press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) and choose Customize the dashboard.

  2. Pick a layout

    On the Layout tab, choose Sidebar, Rail, Top bar, Split, Focus or Tablet / Kiosk. The dashboard changes as you click, so you can try each one.

  3. Or start from a preset

    Presets change several settings at once: Studio (split layout, Midnight Blue), Terminal (icon rail, green on black, monospace), Minimal, Kiosk for a wall tablet, and Paper with serif type.

  4. Choose a theme

    On the Theme tab, pick one of the 13 themes, from Homedroid Dark and OLED Black to Paper and High Contrast. Choose Follow the system to switch between a light and a dark theme with your computer, and set the accent colour, density and corner style.

  5. Change the fonts

    On the Fonts tab, choose the interface, heading and code fonts (System, Humanist, Geometric, Rounded, Classic serif or Monospace, or an installed font by name) and the text size.

  6. Arrange the overview

    On the Widgets tab, add, remove and resize the widgets on the Overview page. Navigation lets you hide pages you never use.

Good to know

  • Tablet / Kiosk is made for a wall tablet: big touch tiles, and a screensaver after five minutes. Tune it, and the OLED care options, on the Theme tab.
  • OLED Black saves power on phones and tablets with OLED screens. Turn on pixel shift under OLED care if the dashboard stays on screen for hours.
  • The phone app has its own themes (Auto, Light, Dark and OLED) in Settings → Display.
