Dock mode: an Android standby clock for your server phone.

Dock mode turns your charging Android phone into a standby clock with live server vitals. Choose one of five faces, add useful tiles, and let the server keep working behind the clock.

Set up an Android standby clock

Open Dock mode from Homedroid’s Tools. Choose a clock face and the tiles you want: server status, battery, CPU, memory, storage, network, uptime, Tailscale or the next alarm. Dock can open while charging or run as Android’s screen saver.

Five faces, live tiles and a night mode

Choose a face to suit the desk or bedside table. A deep-red night mode reduces glare. The clock follows your 12/24-hour preference, with optional seconds. Wallpaper colours are available on Android 12 and later.

OLED care is built in

Pixel shifts, clock-and-tile swaps, dimming and screen-off hours reduce the time the same bright pixels stay lit. They do not make OLED burn-in impossible. Use lower brightness and screen-off hours when you do not need the clock.

Widgets for the home screen

The redesigned status widget comes in four sizes. A separate clock widget opens Dock mode. You can leave the clock closed and still see your server from the launcher.

Keep the phone cool

Use a healthy battery and a ventilated spot. A charge limit, when provided by your phone manufacturer, helps avoid sitting at full charge. Homedroid does not control charging without root. See the battery, temperature and hardware FAQ.
