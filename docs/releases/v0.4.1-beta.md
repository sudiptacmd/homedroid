First public beta of **Homedroid** (formerly Lindroid): turn an old Android phone into a home server, no root.

**Which APK?** Most phones: `arm64-v8a`. Old 32-bit phones: `armeabi-v7a`. Emulators and Chromebooks: `x86_64`. Not sure: `universal`. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS`.

### What's in it
- **Modules you can add, remove and turn on or off:** SSH & SFTP, web hosting (Caddy), Cloudflare Tunnel, Jellyfin, qBittorrent, Home Assistant and Immich.
- **Web dashboard** on port 8800: phone health, services, logs, and deployments from GitHub (Node.js, Python, static sites) with optional auto-deploy.
- **Jellyfin hardware transcoding** through the phone's video encoder (MediaCodec).
- **`torrent <magnet>` over SSH:** in-order downloads into the media library, then seeding.
- **Per-app storage:** inside the app, internal storage, SD card or USB drive.
- **Immich from its official container images**, pulled and run without Docker.

### Known limitations
Tested on emulated Android 10 and 16; the first real-device pilot is in progress. See
[Known limitations](https://github.com/sudiptacmd/homedroid#known-limitations) in the README.

### Upgrading from a Lindroid test build
The package name changed (`dev.lindroid` → `dev.homedroid`), so Homedroid installs as a new app. Uninstall Lindroid after moving anything you need.
