# Homedroid 0.9.1 beta

**Update from 0.9.0 if you use AI on the phone:** in 0.9.0 the model on the phone never became
ready, and the idle model could keep the CPU busy and drain the battery. Both are fixed.

## Fixed

- **On-phone AI works.** Android blocked Homedroid from talking to its own model server, so
  the model stayed "loading" and chat, the AI API and routines failed. The same block hit
  Ollama or LM Studio on your network, `http://` news feeds and self-hosted ntfy. If a model
  isn't ready, the AI page now says why.
- **No more battery drain from an idle model.** llama.cpp waited for work by spinning the CPU;
  on a test phone it kept most cores busy with the screen off. It now sleeps when idle and lets
  go of the model after 10 idle minutes (it reloads in a few seconds when needed).
- Routines cover every source you give them, also with small models.

## New

- **llama.cpp built for Android.** The AI runtime is now built by this project and downloaded
  from the release when you install it (about 17 MB), checked against the version that comes
  with the app. It runs directly on Android, so AI no longer needs Alpine Linux.
- **GPU or CPU.** *Run on: Auto / CPU / GPU*, and a **Benchmark** that measures both; Auto uses
  the faster. Vulkan works with most phone GPUs, OpenCL with Qualcomm Adreno. Older GPU drivers
  can crash llama.cpp: the benchmark notices and Homedroid then stays on the CPU. On our test
  phone (Mali-G72) the GPU crashed, and the CPU wrote 11 tokens/s with Qwen2.5 0.5B.
- **Model recommendations.** The AI page recommends the best model your phone runs well, from
  its memory, free space and, after a benchmark, its measured speed; every other model shows
  why it wasn't picked or how fast it would be.
- **Home-screen widget.** *Homedroid status* shows every phone in your cluster (online,
  battery, temperature) and the services running, refreshed every minute. Tap it to open the
  dashboard.
- Updated architecture diagram.

After updating, open **AI → On this phone** and tap **Install** once to get the new runtime;
your downloaded models are kept. Then run the **Benchmark** for the recommendation.
