**Which APK?** Most phones: `arm64-v8a`. Old 32-bit phones: `armeabi-v7a`. Emulators and Chromebooks: `x86_64`. Not sure: `universal`. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS`. Installs over 0.4.1 and keeps your data.

### New in the dashboard
- **Redesign.** Sidebar on computers and bottom tabs on phones, light/dark/auto theme, switches for modules, in-page dialogs. Lighter too: views only redraw when something changed, only the open tab polls (never in a hidden browser tab), and the page is gzipped and cached.
- **Live load.** CPU load, temperature and upload/download speed beside battery, memory and storage, with small graphs.
- **Files.** Browse the media and photo libraries, the SSH home and website, and phone storage, SD cards and USB drives. Upload files or whole folders (drag and drop), download files or folders (zip), open videos, pictures and PDFs in the browser, rename, move and delete. Transfers stream to disk, so multi-GB files work.
- **Terminal.** A shell in the browser, in the phone's shell or straight into Alpine Linux.
- **SSH access.** Add and remove SSH keys; a log of SSH and terminal sessions (who, from where, how long, what they ran, and rejected logins); the command history of the phone shell and of Alpine.

### Apps
- **Clear data** resets an app like a fresh install. **Remove** can also delete its settings and database. The media or photo library is only deleted when ticked separately, and the dialog says which other apps use it.
- **qBittorrent downloads show up in Jellyfin.** Installing both adds a *Downloads* library that picks up new files within a minute.

### Fixes
- Closing an SSH connection now ends its shell. Android starts apps with SIGHUP ignored, so shells survived their hangup.

### Known limitations
Android doesn't let apps read system-wide CPU load, so the dashboard shows the load of Homedroid's own processes (all the servers). See [Known limitations](https://github.com/sudiptacmd/homedroid#known-limitations).
