# Homedroid 0.9.3 beta

**Sturdier clusters, a new walkthrough and a home on the web.** This release fixes two
cluster problems found while testing a phone together with an emulator, and comes with a
fresh walkthrough, a rewritten README and the new site at
[sudiptacmd.github.io/homedroid](https://sudiptacmd.github.io/homedroid/).

## Fixed

- **Phones behind a port forward or relay keep their real address.** A request that reached
  a phone through a local forward seemed to come from `127.0.0.1`; the cluster took that as
  the other phone's new address and then tried to reach itself ("this is not the phone that
  was paired"). Loopback sources no longer change a member's address or become a pairing
  address.
- **Leaving or pairing survives a sudden power cut.** Cluster membership and the phone's
  cluster identity are now flushed to disk before they replace the old file, so a phone that
  loses power right after leaving a cluster no longer comes back still listing its old
  partners.

## Verified on real hardware

- **USB drives and microSD cards as network storage.** With a card attached, it appears in
  the dashboard's **Files** and in each app's **Storage location** picker: browse, upload,
  download (with seeking), zip folders, rename, move and delete. Removing the card makes the
  location disappear cleanly; nothing is written to internal storage instead.
- **Clusters across versions.** A 0.9.1 phone and a 0.9.2 phone paired, showed each other's
  health, and copied and moved folders both ways with matching checksums.

Your installed apps, libraries and configuration are kept when updating.

This is a prerelease. The tag `v0.9.3-beta` uses the existing release workflow to build and
publish signed APKs for arm64, 32-bit ARM, x86_64 and universal, the AI runtime archives,
and `SHA256SUMS`.
