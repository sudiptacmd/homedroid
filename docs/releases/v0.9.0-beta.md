# Homedroid 0.9.0 beta

Two big additions: several phones working as one, and AI on your phones.

## Cluster: several phones, one dashboard

- **Add another phone** from **Cluster → Add a phone**. Phones on the same Wi-Fi find each
  other automatically; you can also enter an address (works over Tailscale too). Both
  phones show the same 6-digit code; approve the request on the phone being added, from
  its notification or its dashboard.
- **Manage any phone from any phone.** A phone switcher in the dashboard turns every page
  (Overview, Modules, Deployments, Files, Terminal, IPCam, AI, Settings) to the phone you
  pick. The Cluster page shows each phone's battery, temperature, CPU, memory and storage.
- **Choose where each app runs.** The *Where apps run* table shows every app and deployment
  on every phone. **Move** an app to another phone and its settings and database go with
  it, and its library too if you choose; deployments are rebuilt from Git on the new
  phone. If a move fails, the app is turned back on where it was.
- **All cameras on one page**, with a live view from every phone and the latest captures
  from all of them.
- **Every phone's storage in Files.** Browse any phone's locations, and copy or move files
  and folders between phones; they go straight from phone to phone.

Phones talk to each other over encrypted connections (mutual TLS on port 8801), and each
phone only trusts the others it was paired with. A paired phone can fully control the
others, so give every phone's dashboard a strong password. Apps on one phone can't use
another phone's storage directly (that needs root); put an app on the phone that holds
its files instead. There is no automatic failover yet.

## AI

- **Models on your phone.** Turn on the AI module, install llama.cpp (a few tens of MB) and pick a
  model: Qwen2.5, Llama 3.2 or Gemma 3, with a recommendation for your phone's memory.
  Downloads resume and are checked; any Hugging Face `.gguf` link works too. 64-bit phones.
- **Cloud services.** Connect OpenAI, Google Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible service
  (OpenRouter, Groq, or Ollama/LM Studio on your computer). Keys are stored only on the
  phone. Optionally fall back to a cloud model when the phone is too hot or its model isn't
  running.
- **Chat** in the dashboard, with every answer labelled *on this phone* or *cloud*.
- **An API for your other apps**: `http://<phone>:8090/v1`, OpenAI-compatible, with its own
  key. Home Assistant, Open WebUI or your scripts can use the phone's model and your cloud
  models without ever seeing your cloud keys.
- **Routines**: scheduled briefs. Read news feeds, web pages, the weather, your phones'
  health and unread email (IMAP with an app password), then summarize them and read the
  brief aloud on any phone, keep it in *Briefs*, or push it to your phone with ntfy or
  Telegram. Templates for a morning brief, an inbox skim, a news digest and a phone check.
  Routines only read: nothing is marked as read, and email is only sent to a cloud model
  in routines where you allow it.

See the [security review](https://github.com/sudiptacmd/homedroid/blob/main/docs/SECURITY-REVIEW-0.9.0.md)
for what the new features expose and how they are protected.

here or upload it through dashboard App updates.
