# Homedroid 0.9.7 beta

**A layout polish for the web dashboard.** Nothing changes in how Homedroid works; the dashboard
just uses its space better.

## Improved

- **The sidebar scrollbar.** In the slim Rail (and the collapsed sidebar) the menu showed a thick
  classic scrollbar with arrow buttons. It is hidden now. A soft fade on the edge that is cut off
  and a small chevron button show that there is more to scroll, and the chevron scrolls the menu
  down. The page you open scrolls into view in the menu.
- **Wide screens.** Content is centred and can be wider (up to 1640 px) instead of sticking to
  the left. The Overview's *List* style, used by the Terminal theme, no longer stops at 820 px:
  it fills the page, and on large screens its vitals sit in two columns.
- **Services flow into columns** on wide screens instead of one stretched row each.

## Fixed

- A 1 px scrollbar on pages shorter than the window, caused by a hidden screen-reader region.

## Checked

Rail, Sidebar, Top bar, Split, Focus and Kiosk layouts, the Cards, List, Big and Bento Overview
styles, at 1920, 1440, 1366, 1280, 1024 and phone widths.
