Phones in the cluster

Where apps run

Move an app to run it on another phone: its settings and database go with it, and its library if you choose. Deployments are rebuilt from Git on the new phone.
Phones find each other on the same Wi-Fi network. Traffic between them is encrypted, and each phone only trusts the others it was paired with.

Wake on LAN

Wake computers at home from anywhere: the phone sends the magic packet on your network.
Getting a computer ready for Wake on LAN
  • Turn on Wake on LAN (sometimes Power on by PCI-E) in the computer's BIOS or UEFI settings.
  • Windows: in Device Manager, open the network adapter's Power Management and Advanced tabs and allow Wake on Magic Packet. Turning off Fast startup helps a shut-down PC wake.
  • Linux: ethtool -s <interface> wol g. Mac: Wake for network access in Energy settings.
  • Use a network cable if you can; few computers wake over Wi-Fi. The MAC address is the wired adapter's (ipconfig /all, ip link).
  • From scripts and phone shortcuts: POST /api/wol/wake with {"id": "…"} or {"mac": "…"} and the dashboard password as a Bearer token.

AI

Chat with a model on this phone or a cloud service you connect, and use them from your other apps.

Routines

Scheduled tasks: read news, the weather, your phones and unread email, then brief you by voice, in Briefs below, or as a push to your phone. Routines only read; they can't send email or act for you.

Briefs

Email and push settings for routines
Email is read over IMAP with an app password (Gmail, Outlook, iCloud and Fastmail all offer one in their security settings). Nothing is marked as read, and email only goes to cloud models in routines where you allow it.
Push to your own phone, also away from home: ntfy (install the ntfy app and subscribe to a hard-to-guess topic) or a Telegram bot (create one with @BotFather, then message it once; the chat id is your user id, from @userinfobot).

On this phone

Models run on the phone's CPU with llama.cpp. Bigger models answer better but slower, use more memory and make the phone warm. You can also upload a .gguf file to AI models in Files.

Cloud services

Firewall
Choose which addresses may open the dashboard and your web sites.

      

        
Your address…

          
How rules are checked, in plain words:

          
  1. This phone itself (and a tunnel running on it) is always let in, so you can’t lock yourself out of the phone.
    2. 
            
  2. If a Deny rule matches a visitor’s address, they are blocked. Deny always wins, so the order of the rules doesn’t matter.
    3. 
            
  3. If there are Allow rules for a part (dashboard or web sites), only addresses matching one of them get in. With no Allow rules, everyone who isn’t denied gets in.

Your computers

      

      
Use the phone as a webcam
        
Pair a computer, then on the phone open MacroPads → Your computers and choose Enable Desktop webcam (it asks for camera permission and keeps a notification while it is ready). Then pick the phone as the camera in Homedroid Desktop. Stop IPCam capture first: only one of them can use the camera at a time. The webcam can’t be turned on from this dashboard because the phone has to grant camera access itself.
