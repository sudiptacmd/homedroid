Homedroid

Cluster

Several phones, one dashboard. Each phone runs its own modules; manage any of them from here.

This phone

Phones in the cluster

Where apps run

Move an app to run it on another phone: its settings and database go with it, and its library if you choose. Deployments are rebuilt from Git on the new phone.
Phones find each other on the same Wi-Fi network. Traffic between them is encrypted, and each phone only trusts the others it was paired with.

AI

Chat with a model on this phone or a cloud service you connect, and use them from your other apps.

Routines

Scheduled tasks: read news, the weather, your phones and unread email, then brief you by voice, in Briefs below, or as a push to your phone. Routines only read; they can't send email or act for you.

Briefs

Email and push settings for routines
Email is read over IMAP with an app password (Gmail, Outlook, iCloud and Fastmail all offer one in their security settings). Nothing is marked as read, and email only goes to cloud models in routines where you allow it.
Push to your own phone, also away from home: ntfy (install the ntfy app and subscribe to a hard-to-guess topic) or a Telegram bot (create one with @BotFather, then message it once; the chat id is your user id, from @userinfobot).

On this phone

Models run on the phone's CPU with llama.cpp. Bigger models answer better but slower, use more memory and make the phone warm. You can also upload a .gguf file to AI models in Files.

Cloud services

The key is checked, then stored only on this phone; the dashboard never shows it again. Cloud models send your messages to that company.

IPCam

Cameras, microphone and announcements from your phone.

Enable IPCam access in the phone app, then you can turn the display off. Android privacy indicators stay active. Video and microphone audio are saved separately.

Camera settings

Settings are saved on the phone separately for each camera. One camera can monitor continuously at a time, even with this page closed. Stop monitoring before taking a manual photo or video. Supported hardware sizes and frame rates may differ from your preset.

Capture storage

Includes videos, photos and microphone recordings. When space fills up, the phone deletes the oldest completed captures automatically. Active recordings are protected. Reducing the limit also removes old captures; download anything you want to keep first.

Microphone

Use Stop microphone to end remote access; Stop listening only mutes this browser. Audio recordings stop after 30 minutes. Use headphones when listening to avoid feedback.

Announce

Uses the phone’s text-to-speech voice, media volume and current audio output.

Saved captures

Latest 100 captures. Saved in the app’s camera folder; download files to keep a copy. Clip duration follows that camera’s saved settings. Continued motion starts a new clip when the maximum length is reached.
